Childcare Program Coordinator

Company: Bright Future Childcare Center

We are looking for a Childcare Program Coordinator to support
children, families, and staff in our childcare programs.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Early Childhood Development, Elementary Education,
  Child Development, or a related field.
- Experience working with children and families.
- Experience coordinating childcare or educational activities.
- Strong communication and organizational skills.
- Ability to maintain client or student records.
- Ability to work with teachers, counselors, families, and healthcare
  professionals.

Preferred:
- Experience working with special needs children.
- Experience coordinating volunteers or staff.
- Experience supporting families with healthcare or financial assistance.
- Experience planning activities for children.

Responsibilities:
- Coordinate daily childcare activities.
- Support teachers and counselors.
- Communicate with children and families.
- Maintain client and student records.
- Coordinate with healthcare professionals when required.
- Support special needs children and their families.
- Assist with planning educational and recreational activities.
